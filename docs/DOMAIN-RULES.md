# TFA Domain Rules Used by TFA-OS

This is a technical/domain summary of established rules. The Constitution remains authoritative for the actual rule text.

## Access

- Visitors can read published public TFA information but cannot comment or participate.
- Anyone may register.
- Registered Users can comment and participate in TCL.
- TFC participation requires Manager status.
- Managers operate one Club only.
- President/Admin is the highest administrative role, subject to the Constitution.

## Clubs

- Club identity is permanent under the constitutional rules.
- Clubs retain history when Managers leave.
- An inactive Club is not deleted.
- A future approved Manager can take over an available/inactive Club according to the applicable TFA process.
- Retained Club TCP remains with the Club.

## Players

- TFA maintains an official Player Pool.
- Player, Player Card, and Player Right are distinct concepts.
- A Club holds Player Rights.
- Player Card eligibility must follow TFA rules.
- MSV is a market/value mechanism and is not a player tier.

## Contracts and market

The system must be capable of representing contracts and the constitutional market lifecycle, including transfers, renewals, releases, waivers, auctions, free agents, and Player Right loans.

## Player Right loans

A loan temporarily places a Player Right with another Club under a registered loan arrangement. Ownership/right history remains traceable to the parent Club. This is not a TCP loan or Treasury financing feature.

## TCL and TFC

TCL and TFC are separate competitions with separate participation rules. Registered Users can participate in TCL; TFC requires Manager status.

## Match results

Where two Manager submissions are required, they are tied to the same fixture. Matching scores can be automatically verified and published. Conflicts are held for President resolution. Official corrections remain auditable.

## TCP

TCP is recorded through Club/Treasury accounts and transactions. The ledger is authoritative. Automated rewards and other deterministic transactions should be generated by domain logic. Administrative adjustments require authorization and audit.

## Records

TFA-OS must preserve official history. Corrections should add a traceable correction/adjustment rather than silently destroying the previous state.

## Constitutional caution

This summary is intentionally not an exhaustive replacement for the Constitution. When a detailed rule is required for implementation, consult the authoritative constitutional source and record the relevant rule in the technical specification before coding.
